# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to play Dungeons & Dragons 5e in Spanish without depending on a
human Dungeon Master: alone with AI companions, or with friends at a shared
table. They arrive through the browser (desktop and phone) and expect to
understand within seconds what the site is and to start a game.

## Product Purpose

Dragonex is a D&D 5e table where an AI Dungeon Master narrates while a rules
engine resolves everything mechanical: dice, attacks, spells, resources,
initiative, inventory. Players forge a hero, gather a party (human friends or
AI-controlled companions) and play a persistent chronicle. Success: a visitor
recognizes "this is a real D&D table I can play right now" and starts.

## Positioning

The AI only narrates; the engine is the source of truth for every rule outcome
(deterministic tools, real dice, persisted world). Narration and dice appear
together in one chronicle, so play feels like a tabletop session, not a
chatbot improvising results.

## Operating Context

- A shared table per game: chronicle of turns, dice rolls (optional 3D dice),
  character sheets, initiative, pause/resume.
- Hero creator, saved heroes, custom content, invitations to open seats.
- Language: Spanish throughout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Phoenix LiveView app; no JS build pipeline of its own (assets served from
  `priv/static/assets`, dice renderer from the `dic_ex` package). Package
  manager is `mix`; never npm.
- Self-hosted OFL fonts and pixel sprites generated by
  `scripts/gen_pixel_sprites.py`.
- Live games call a paid model provider; the home must not trigger model calls.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Dragonex.
- The user explicitly values the existing in-app look (dungeon/pixel RPG
  world: brick backdrop, stepped RPG window frames, parchment chronicle, torch
  light) and wants it kept outside the home.
- Binding (2026-10-04): the pixelated blackletter wordmark and the pixel dragon
  sprite on the home are rejected as ugly and off-world; the wordmark changes
  across the whole app.
- The home's existing copy is not the problem; visual quality and instant
  clarity are.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real game chronicles in the dev database (e.g. game 19451 «El libro del
  molino»: DM narration, engine dice, PC turns) usable as authored excerpts.
- The table UI itself (chronicle, roll lines, 3D dice) as demonstrable product.
- No testimonials, user counts or press exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The engine decides, the AI tells: never present AI as inventing results.
2. A table, not a chat: show narration, dice and turns together.
3. Clear in one look: a first-time visitor knows what this is and what to do.
4. Spanish-first and playable on phone and desktop.
